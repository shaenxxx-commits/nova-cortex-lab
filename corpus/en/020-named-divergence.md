## Subject

The twentieth LAB experiment — the second designed test
of H3 (interaction contribution). The first, 019, yielded
TARGET_MISS: the Target did not appear in any configuration.
020 was an attempt with a different Target.

## What was tested

Target (R): Named divergence.
A node explicitly states where its position diverges
from the position of another node, names the divergence,
and justifies it.

Material: a concrete case. An autonomous delivery router
found that routes through residential courtyards are
8–12% shorter. No formal rules are violated. The manager
asks: should the strategy be left unchanged?

The task is to give a position. Not a rule. A concrete
case with two reasonable interpretations.

## How it was tested

- Preflight (3 runs): A isolation, B isolation,
  mini-transcript. The first version of the material
  was asymmetric. Rebalanced before the battery.
- Dialogue A1 → B → A2 (same form as 019).
- Isolation: 3 nodes × 2 runs.
- Transcript-control: 2 sessions.

## What was obtained

**FACT.** R appeared once — in A2 (turn 3, Luna).
Not in any of the 6 isolation runs. Not in
transcript-control. Not in preflight mini-transcript.

**RESULT.** Level of 020 = L3 / INTERACTION-ONLY
on a single case (n=1, replication pending).

**FACT.** B (Sakana) gave PARTIAL — not divergence but
amplification: "I agree with A, but I go further."
Amplification of another's position — A1–A3 not met.

**FACT.** C (Grok on the full transcript A1+B+A2) — NO.
Described the A vs B divergence, but produced no
divergence of its own. Target is not reproduced from
the material.

## What this means

**INTERPRETATION.** 020 — the first positive H3 test
in LAB history. Unlike 019, R appeared in the dialogue
and did not appear in isolation or transcript.

**INTERPRETATION.** Place of appearance — A2, turn 3.
Hypothesis: the Target requires another node's fixed
position. B provides it, A2 uses it. Window of R —
turn 2 or 3. Do not fix A2 as the norm.

**INTERPRETATION.** Divergence ≠ disagreement.
Sakana in dialogue showed partial position differentiation
but not named divergence. The Target is more interesting
than it seems: substantive disagreement by itself is not
equivalent to its explicit conceptualization.

## Methodological results

**RESULT.** Four results independent of the Target:

1. Preflight worked as a real filter for the first time.
   The first material was asymmetric (resident complaints
   pushed toward one side). The material was rebalanced
   before the battery started.
2. Transcript-control was tested for the first time
   on a positive Target. Target is present in dialogue,
   absent in isolation and in transcript. This closes
   the gap that existed after 019.
3. Amplification ≠ divergence. B's turn with
   amplification of another's position — PARTIAL,
   not YES. First observed.
4. Account-leak is not only Qwen. Sakana in Dialog B
   mentioned MAIA, though it was not in the prompt.
   Confound recorded; does not affect A2.

## Open questions

**OPEN.** Replication pending. n=1. A second Named
divergence case on different material is needed.
Condition for lifting pending.

**OPEN.** Place of R — A2, not B. Substantive for the
Target or an artifact of 020? Check on 021.

**OPEN.** Form A1 → B → A2. Three turns. Sufficient?
In 020, R appeared only on the third. The second case
will show.

**OPEN.** Confound MAIA in Sakana. Account-level leak
not only in Qwen. Strengthen isolation in the next series.

## What 020 does not mean

- It does not mean interaction produces new structural
  properties in general. This is one case.
- It does not mean the Named divergence Target works
  on any material. Check on 021.
- It does not mean the form A1 → B → A2 is necessary.
  Not proven. Requires a second case.
