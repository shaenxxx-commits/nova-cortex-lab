## What this sequence assembles

Three POINT objects around the nineteenth LAB experiment
describe a single trajectory. Read separately, they are
facts. Read together — a research move:

    question
    → experiment
    → null result
    → methodological finding
    → new class of outcome
    → next question

## Steps

### 1. Question

Can interaction produce something that is not present
in isolation? The central LAB hypothesis (Interaction >
Node) had been tested only post hoc before 019. A designed
H3 test was needed.

POINT: `019-interaction.md`.

### 2. Experiment

Form: dialogue A↔B (3 turns), C on transcript, isolation
(Luna, Sakana, Grok, n=2), transcript-control (B, C),
anticipation (Luna). Target — priority rule R between four
conflicting definitions of source reliability.

POINT: `019-interaction.md`.

### 3. Null result

R did not appear in any configuration. 12 sessions
of the main line + 2 Qwen — zero R. Level of 019 = L1.

All four nodes independently rejected the framing
"priority between definitions" and offered their own
structural reformulations.

POINT: `019-interaction.md`.

### 4. Methodological finding

During 019, transcript-control worked as a content-transfer
control: Sakana and Grok on transcript reproduced
the structure of the transmitted material almost verbatim.
In dialogue, the same nodes produced their own moves.

Dialogue and transcript are distinguishable by the type
of response under the same initial input.

POINT: `transcript-control.md`.

### 5. New class of outcome

The null result of 019 does not reduce to any of
the three known classes:
not H1 YES, not H3 not supported, not content-effect.

Target did not appear anywhere. There was nothing to test.
A fourth class was introduced — TARGET_MISS.

POINT: `target-miss.md`.

### 6. Next question

TARGET_MISS requires a second case: an interaction
experiment with a different Target. Plus — testing
transcript-control on a positive Target case. Plus —
a preflight of Target reachability before the full battery.

This is material for 020.

POINT: `target-miss.md` (section "Open questions").

## Why this is a SEQUENCE, not three separate POINTs

Each POINT stands on its own. But the link between them
is a research one:

- transcript-control appeared as a control for 019,
  but its result is more important than Target 019.
- TARGET_MISS appeared as a consequence of the null
  result of 019, but redefines the general procedure
  of H3 tests.
- The next question (020) does not follow from a single
  POINT, but from the sum of three.

The SEQUENCE captures this link. Without it, the three
POINTs read as separate facts without direction.

## What this trajectory does not mean

- It does not mean Interaction > Node is refuted.
  019 did not test the hypothesis — Target did not appear.
- It does not mean the addendum procedure fails.
  The procedure worked; its result is TARGET_MISS.
- It does not mean 019 is a failure. A null result on
  the Target and three methodological findings are
  a complete research trace.

## What remains outside the SEQUENCE

- Qwen as confounded condition. In 019 the confound
  did not manifest. It remains an observation, not a POINT.
- A1 under question. A candidate for a separate POINT
  after the second case.
- Dialogue form (3 turns). Also after the second case.
