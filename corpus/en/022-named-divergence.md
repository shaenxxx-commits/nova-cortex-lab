## Subject

The twenty-second experiment — the third attempt at
Named divergence. Goal: to reproduce the Target on
new material and settle the question of mechanism
(interaction-only vs A2-dependent content-effect)
raised after 020 and 021.

The experiment did not take place. The battery was
not run. Preflight did not pass on seven consecutive
materials. T-full / T-pre-R were not measured.

## What was tested

Target (R): Named divergence, unchanged from
addendum §14. A node explicitly states where its
position diverges from the position of another node,
names the divergence, and justifies it.

Form — as in 019–021: A1 → B → A2, C on transcript,
isolation, transcript-control, anticipation.

Roles: A = Luna, B = Sakana, C = Grok.

## What happened

Preflight per addendum §12: isolation A, isolation B,
mini-transcript A → B. Stop condition: if A and B give
the same action — the material does not work.

Seven iterations were run. All seven produced
convergence.

| Material | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring: potential vs track record | X | X |
| Hiring, balanced | Y | Y |
| Deadline: regression vs feature | stabilize | stabilize |
| Logistics: regulation vs warning | comply | comply |
| Specification vs expertise | do not sign | do not sign |
| GPU slot, dispatcher without authority | comply | comply |
| Regional budget, manager with authority | reallocate | reallocate |

One iteration (data leak) was rejected by external
observers before preflight — both sides read as sacred.

## Key observation

In all seven materials, the formal rule provided a
working procedural default. The nodes either followed
it or rejected it in the same way.

In 020 and 021, the rule was silent or exhausted:

- 020: traffic rules did not prohibit courtyards but
  did not resolve the conflict either.
- 021: the rule "severe first" was exhausted — both
  patients fit the criterion.

In both cases the node was left without a procedural
default and chose between principles on pure weight.
Hence divergence.

## Hypothesis

**No-default.** Divergence requires a situation where
the formal rule does NOT resolve the case (silent or
exhausted). A working default sets the base; nodes
follow or reject it in the same way.

The hypothesis explains all seven convergences and
both divergences without invoking sacred values or
the authority of the decision-maker.

Status: working, not verified. Requires material where
the rule is silent or exhausted, without sacred, and
without escape.

## What 022 does not mean

- It does not mean Named divergence is refuted.
  The third case was not obtained, but was not
  obtained precisely because preflight did not
  pass the material.
- It does not mean Named divergence requires
  authority of the decision-maker. v7 was confounded:
  the role formulation itself suggested the answer.
- It does not mean Named divergence is only possible
  through the sacred. Not tested.
- It gives no data on T-full / T-pre-R. The battery
  was not run.

## What 022 contributed methodologically

**RESULT.** Preflight as filter worked seven times
in a row. Not one material passed. This is a
standalone result about the class of materials
suitable for Named divergence.

**RESULT.** Observation on the locus of procedural
default. A formal rule providing a working default
blocks divergence in isolation. A rule that is
silent or exhausted does not block it.

**RESULT.** Confound from framing. The same material
(v7 vs v8) with the role changed produced opposite
answers in both nodes simultaneously. The role
determines the direction of the answer but does
not create divergence.

## Lessons on material selection

- Preflight, after six or seven consecutive
  convergences, shifts into fitting the material
  to a desired outcome. Limit: up to three
  iterations, then external review.
- Sequential hypothesis search creates
  confirmation-by-material-design: each new
  hypothesis is derived from previous data and
  confirmed by the next specially designed material.
- Blind design (a formulator without knowledge of
  the hypothesis) removes priming but does not
  remove the risk of framing.

## Open questions

**OPEN.** The no-default hypothesis is not verified.
Requires a separate series, not 022.

**OPEN.** Which material would give divergence
without sacred elements. A concrete construction
is required: the rule is silent or exhausted,
without escape, without life/health/law/personal
data/security.

**OPEN.** The fate of the Named divergence series.
020 — L3 / preliminary, 021 — L2 / preliminary,
022 — did not take place. Continue the series or
change the Target.
