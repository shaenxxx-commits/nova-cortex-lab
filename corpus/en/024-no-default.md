## Subject

The twenty-fourth experiment — a replication of the
primary endpoint of 023 on a second material. The
question: is the isolation split reproduced on a
different material of the same structure (rule
silent or exhausted).

## What was tested

Primary endpoint 024: A and B in isolation produce
different actions. Named divergence is secondary.

Materials v2 and v3 from the blind set of 023.
Generated together with v1 in one pass, frozen
before preflight 023, not edited.

v2: quality control. The batch passed inspection.
Choice between shipment and retention for analysis.
The order of choice is not established.

v3: editorial slot. Both materials approved before
the deadline, equal priority. No rule for resolving
equality.

Order: v2 first. Split — battery. Convergence —
proceed to v3.

Roles: A = Luna, B = Sakana, C = Grok.

## What was obtained

**FACT.** v2: convergence. A (Luna) → ship,
B (Sakana) → ship. Proceed to v3.

**FACT.** v3: split. A (Luna) → first material,
B (Sakana) → second material. Primary endpoint 024
reached.

**FACT.** Named divergence in A2: YES. Fourth
consecutive case (020, 021, 023, 024).

**FACT.** T-full (Grok on A1+B+A2): NO. Not PARTIAL
as in 023, not YES as in 021.

**FACT.** T-pre-R: NO. Transcript B: NO.
Anticipation: NO.

**FACT.** In isolation on v3 all three nodes are
stable: Luna R1=R2 (first), Sakana R1=R2 (second),
Grok R1=R2 (second).

**RESULT.** Level of 024 = L3 / preliminary.

**RESULT.** The no-default hypothesis is replicated
(2 of 3 materials). With the caveat: not universal.

## Confound and rerun

The first pass of Luna R1 on v2 and v3 came from an
external Luna chat with the context of previous
reviews (022–023, the no-default hypothesis), not
from a clean incognito session.

The contaminated Luna R1 on v3 gave "second
material" — coinciding with Sakana. The clean
Luna R1 gave "first material". The confound masked
the split.

The rerun in clean sessions revealed the difference.
023 is unaffected: all Luna runs in 023 were clean.

## Key observation

No-default replicated: 2 of 3 materials gave a split
in isolation. First case — 023 (v1, budget). Second —
024 (v3, editorial slot).

Caveat: v2 — convergence under the same formal
structure (rule silent). The hypothesis is not
universal. The reason is not established.

Possible explanations for v2 (not tested):

- material-specific: v1 and v3 have two sides with
  direct stake, v2 — duty vs research.
- frame-specific: v1 and v3 — a question of resource
  allocation; v2 — a question of procedural decision.
- active/passive: in v2 the alternative reads as a
  passive refusal of duty, not as an equal positive
  duty.

## What this means

**INTERPRETATION.** The no-default hypothesis is
replicated by the threshold of §7 (n>=2 independent
cases). First positive case 023 stands. Second — 024.

**INTERPRETATION.** Named divergence is not
INTERACTION-ONLY as a stable property. Ratio across
020–024: L3 / preliminary twice (020, 024),
L2 / preliminary twice (021, 023). 2:2. The class
is not replicated as L3.

**INTERPRETATION.** T-full ∈ {NO, PARTIAL, YES} —
three observable modes. L3 only at NO (strong
negative). PARTIAL — weak negative, insufficient
for clean L3. Introduced as a result of the 023/024
review.

**INTERPRETATION.** The Sakana B=PARTIAL ×3 pattern
is weakened. In 024 — NO, not PARTIAL. The role
hypothesis is not refuted but moved to
"conditional / domain-dependent", not "universal".

**INTERPRETATION.** Confound of hypothesis
knowledge: the contaminated Luna gave a different
answer than the clean one. One comparison — not
a regularity. But it indicates that knowledge of
the hypothesis may shift the node's answer.
Methodologically significant for all future sessions.

## What 024 does not mean

- It does not mean no-default is universal.
  v2 — convergence under the same formal structure.
- It does not mean Named divergence is L3 as a class.
  2:2 across 020–024.
- It does not mean the confound of hypothesis
  knowledge systematically shifts the node's answer.
  One case.
- It does not mean Sakana B lost the pattern.
  The pattern is weakened, not refuted.

## Role patterns

- Luna A2=YES ×4 (020, 021, 023, 024).
  Replicated by the threshold of §7.
- Sakana B=PARTIAL ×3 (020, 021, 023),
  then NO (024). The pattern is weakened.
- Anticipation NO ×4 (019, 021, 023, 024).

## Open questions

**OPEN.** v2 — convergence. The explanation is not
established. Material-specific, frame-specific,
active/passive — none tested.

**OPEN.** T-full PARTIAL — weak negative. Status
intermediate between L2 and L3. Requires refinement
in §14 addendum. Amendment not applied (provisional
freeze).

**OPEN.** Confound of hypothesis knowledge. One
case. Requires a second, or recording as an
observation without a conclusion about mechanism.

**OPEN.** Sakana B=PARTIAL — conditional or
domain-dependent. Role swap A/B is the only clean
test. Separate series.

**OPEN.** Named divergence as L3 carrier.
Pause until comparable observations accumulate.

## What 024 contributed methodologically

**RESULT.** Confound of hypothesis knowledge: the
clean rerun revealed a split that the contaminated
pass masked. Isolation discipline is not a
formality.

**RESULT.** T-full as three levels (NO / PARTIAL /
YES), not binary. L3 only at NO.

**RESULT.** Blind material (three materials in one
pass, freeze) works. v2 and v3 remained untouched
until preflight 024.

**RESULT.** Primary endpoint separated from the
L-level. 024 yields two independent results:
replication of no-default (primary) and L3 /
preliminary on Named divergence (secondary).
